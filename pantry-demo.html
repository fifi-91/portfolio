What’s in your cupboard right now?

A receipt is the only moment your kitchen is ever measured. Everything after that is a guess.

Pantry makes the guess honest: a range, not a date, and sharper with every shop.

The thinking
Problem
You only find out you’ve run out when you reach for it. Pantry apps fix that by asking you to log everything, and nobody keeps it up.
Target user
People who shop weekly or fortnightly and cook at home, whose routine shifts week to week. In a survey of 26 people, 88% ran out of something at least every two weeks.
Competitors
Manual trackers and barcode scanners need constant input. Supermarket apps know what you bought, not what you’ve used. Smart fridges see one shelf, at a price.
Insight
The receipt is the only reliable signal. Between receipts, any app is guessing, so the honest design shows the guess as a range and lets each new receipt sharpen it.
Why I chose it
It’s a digital twin you can feel: small, everyday, and all about designing for uncertainty, the hardest part of any predictive product.

The challenge

Every client wanted its own copilot. Building ten would not scale.

Ten bespoke assistants would have been slow, expensive and inconsistent.

Why it mattered

Every brand wanted GenAI in front of customers, yesterday. Most pilots died as one-offs that nobody trusted. This made GenAI repeatable for the business and trustworthy for customers.

Agent orchestration

Three patterns, one reasoning engine.

The experience had to flex with how the agents worked, and show their reasoning.

Designing for trust

Models are non-deterministic. The interface has to earn trust.

A confident wrong answer is worse than a question. So the copilot asks when unsure and never acts without a yes.

The avatar is the status light

Its spin tells customers how hard the copilot is working, so they know whether to wait, without a progress bar.

The solution

White-label by parameter, not by redesign.

Brand, voice, data, agents and rules: set per client, on the same components.

Prototyped end to end, per client

Design system

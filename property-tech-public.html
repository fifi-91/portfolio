The challenge

People wanted to own a home. The journey got in the way.

Saudi buyers had to navigate sales centres, banks and paperwork to buy a home. Buying took 46 days from lead to sale, and many buyers dropped out before reserving.

Design system

From designing pages to building a system.

I introduced an atomic design system, from atoms and molecules to templates, to replace one-off screens with reusable patterns across products and languages.

A business case, not just a Figma library

  • Make speed and reuse measurable.
  • Give components clear owners and governance.
  • Align Figma and Storybook naming for design-to-code consistency.

With engineering

The design was only finished when the build matched it.

I reviewed working builds with front-end engineers and resolved differences against the designs before release. The team repeated this design QA loop every two weeks.

  1. 01SpecifyComponents and templates with states, rules and both languages.
  2. 02BuildEngineers implement the shared specifications.
  3. 03Review liveDesign QA on working software, with each squad before release.
  4. 04Decide and logFixes and new needs go into the shared system backlog.
The solution

One bilingual system, from first visit to signed agreement.

The system supported a complete home acquisition journey, from browsing and finance estimates to reservation and agreement. Explore the walkthrough and bilingual prototype below.

Arabic as a primary script

  • Type: Arabic-specific typography, not a Latin fallback.
  • Line height: Space for connected letters and diacritics.
  • Mirroring: Reverse navigation and layout selectively.
  • Numbers: Keep prices and numerals readable left to right.

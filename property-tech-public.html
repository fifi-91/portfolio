The challenge

People wanted to own a home. The journey got in the way.

Saudi homeownership rose from about 47% in 2016 to 66% in 2025, on the way to a Vision 2030 target of 70%. Demand was there. But finding, financing and living in a home happened across sales centres, banks, paperwork and separate service channels. Turning a lead into a sale was slow.

My role

One direction across three product streams.

As Associate Design Director I led design for home acquisition, home services and community, owned the design system, and coached the client’s designers. These were the calls that shaped it:

  • One system for three products, not three systems. Products differ in colour and tone, never in how things work.
  • Arabic from the first component, not a translation pass at the end. Every component has a language property.
  • Tokens as the contract with engineering. I worked closely with the front-end team in live build reviews, so the design held up in code.
  • Build the client’s capability, so their team could run and extend the system after we left.
The solution

One bilingual system, from first visit to signed agreement.

2,695+ components were ready for launch. The library grew to 5,066 variants, 1,838 of them in Arabic, with light and dark modes. Here is the home acquisition journey it made possible, and a prototype you can try in both languages.

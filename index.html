How I lead

Hands on, always.

I lead from inside the work: setting direction, reviewing in Figma and designing alongside my team. Craft is how I earn trust.

Design is a business tool.

Every decision ties back to a measurable outcome, whether that’s conversion, adoption or time saved.

Teams that grow.

I give designers ownership of whole problems, not just screens, and create space for honest critique and learning. Outside work, I mentor designers pro bono, because helping people grow is the part of leadership I love most.

AI in the workflow.

I prototype in code with AI tools every day, and help teams use AI to explore more and ship faster, without lowering the bar.

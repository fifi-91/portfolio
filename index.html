About

I’m a product design leader working across enterprise technology, emerging products and independent collaborations.

Originally trained in graphic and publication design at University of the Arts London, I bring an editorial eye to product strategy, experience design and creative technology. My career has taken me from London to Paris and Mexico City. I work in English and French, and I’m open to design leadership roles and ambitious collaborations.
